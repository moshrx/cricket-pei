Privacy & Legal Terms

Effective Date: February 8, 2026

This page summarizes our website and participation terms. It is provided for general information and is not legal advice.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for the 2026 season, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, emergency contact details, and playing experience. This data is used for team organization, insurance administration, safety communication, and league operations.

2. Data Use, Retention & Security

We retain personal data only as long as needed for administration, legal compliance, and league records. We apply reasonable administrative safeguards, but no digital system can be guaranteed fully secure.

Cricket PEI may document matches and community events. By participating in our programs, your image may appear in our Gallery or on our social media platforms. If you need specific content removed for privacy or safety reasons, contact us and include the relevant link or screenshot.

To request assistance, email cricketpei@gmail.com with the page URL, issue description, and preferred contact method. We aim to acknowledge requests within 5 business days.
