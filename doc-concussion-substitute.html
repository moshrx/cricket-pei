Concussion Substitute

Player safety first — protocols for managing concussion injuries during play and provisions for substitute arrangements.

About This Policy

Player safety is paramount at Cricket PEI. The Concussion Substitute Policy outlines protocols for identifying and managing concussion injuries during play, providing provisions for a substitute fielder or batsman while an injured player receives proper medical assessment and care.

This policy aligns with current best practices in sport concussion management and ensures that no player is pressured to continue playing after a suspected concussion. The welfare of our members always comes before the result of any match.

Immediate Removal

Any player suspected of suffering a concussion must be immediately removed from play, with no exceptions during active match conditions.

Medical Assessment

A qualified medical professional must assess the injured player before any return-to-play decision is considered or approved.

Substitute Provisions

An approved concussion substitute may field for the injured player. Specific batting and bowling restrictions apply as outlined in the policy.
